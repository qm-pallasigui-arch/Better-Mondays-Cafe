Inventory Help
- Admins can add, edit, and remove stock items.
- Product batches support SKU + expiry date tracking.
- FEFO is enabled: earliest expiry batches are deducted first.
- Inventory status column includes:
	- LOW STOCK
	- EXPIRING<7D
	- EXPIRED
	- ABC=<A|B|C>
	- EOQ~N (recommended reorder quantity)
- Color cue:
	- Red = expired batch risk
	- Orange = low stock or expiring batch
	- Green = healthy status

ABC Guide
- A: high value/critical inventory focus (top cumulative usage)
- B: medium control priority
- C: routine control priority

EOQ Guide
- EOQ is the suggested reorder quantity minimizing ordering + holding cost.
- Use EOQ as a planning baseline, then adjust with supplier constraints.
